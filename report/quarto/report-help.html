Detectors & event counts

The Reported TE calls by detector table counts rows of TE_INFOS.bed, grouped by their event identifier and type. The current call filters apply. Several calls can occur at one locus; these are not necessarily independent biological alleles.

Detector labelMeaning
SnifflesOUTSIDER calls with Sniffles event IDs. The label covers either supported Sniffles generation; it does not infer its version from the identifier.
TrEMOLOOUTSIDER calls with TrEMOLO. event IDs or the HARD./SOFT. channel IDs, covering direct CIGAR and clipping channels. Columns separate INS, DEL, SOFT and HARD evidence.
AssemblyticsINSIDER calls with Assemblytics event IDs.
Other / unknownIdentifiers that do not establish one of the detector labels above remain explicit.

Insertions include INS/INSERTION and expansion types. Deletions include DEL/DELETION and contraction types. SOFT and HARD are reported separately. INS_DEL is counted once as insertion evidence in a deletion context, not twice.

Raw Sniffles VCF counts, when available, are shown separately: they include SV records before TE annotation and do not follow the interactive call filters. They must not be added to the final reported TE counts.

Call positions by chromosome

Chromosome bars use a shared base-pair scale: shorter chromosomes have shorter bars. Labels show Mb; hovering a bar or size label reveals the exact bp length. Dots represent the selected calls, located at their reported anchor.

The scale stays fixed when changing call filters. Unknown chromosome lengths are explicitly labelled; dashed bars show the observed span, not a claimed reference length.

Calls per genomic window

The heatmap is an additional aggregate view; the individual-call chromosome plot remains unchanged. Choose a window width to group anchors into consecutive zero-based, half-open intervals. Each call belongs to one window; the final chromosome window may be shorter.

Magma colours encode the number of selected calls per window. Pale neutral cells contain no selected calls. This shows counts, not read depth, resident TE abundance or length-normalized density. The colour maximum is calculated from all calls at the current window width, so filtering does not silently rescale the colours.

Hover or focus a cell to see INSIDER, OUTSIDER and unknown-source counts, both selected and before filtering. A blue outline marks Filter affected windows where at least one baseline call was excluded. Unoutlined windows are Unchanged for the current selection. Cells with no selected calls can still be filter affected.

The baseline is the complete reported call table. It does not mean the entire genome was callable, and an empty window does not establish biological absence. Call filters affect this view; resident-table and structure-preview controls are independent.

Frequency versus local depth

The x axis is the depth used by the frequency estimate, and y is TE frequency (%). A high fraction supported by two reads differs from the same fraction supported by 200 reads. The adjustable guides are exploratory aids, not confidence thresholds.

  • OUTSIDER: adjusted read denominator from the same raw/precise frequency table selected for the reported call. This need not equal raw BAM coverage.
  • INSIDER: integer mean of the depths sampled at the two flanks.
  • DEL: frequency describes TE presence, not deletion frequency.

Missing or zero depths, mismatching estimates and invalid counts are omitted from the scatter plot and remain explicit in the export. No denominator is inferred from frequency. These local estimators do not measure fold enrichment against genome-wide coverage.

Discovery · Fragments retained and rejected

BLAST HSPs→ discovery filters→ compatible matches→ grouped copies

A fragment is one local BLAST alignment (HSP), not a whole TE copy. Several fragments can support one copy or competing annotations. Consequently the fragment count can exceed the copy count.

Report labelInterpretation
acceptedThe HSP passes discovery filters. This does not mean a full-length or biologically validated TE. Redundancy handling and chaining occur later.
rejected:aligned_bpThe aligned length is below the discovery minimum.
rejected:identityAlignment identity is below the discovery minimum.
Other rejection reasonsReasons can include chromosome filtering or e-value; combined reasons describe the same rejected fragment, not additional independent fragments.

Discovery summaries refer to the input assembly catalogue. Call-table filters and reporting-tier sliders do not rerun discovery.

Coverage × identity classification

Consensus coverage measures how much of the database TE sequence is covered by the compatible alignment fragments of a match, combining their intervals without counting overlaps twice.

coverage (%) = 100 × union of aligned consensus intervals / TE consensus length

For a 5,000 bp consensus, two nonoverlapping 600 bp pieces give 24% coverage. If they cover exactly the same consensus interval, coverage is only 12%. This is neither the largest fragment alone, nor genomic span, nor read depth.

TierDecision order
full_lengthGlobal coverage reaches the full-length threshold AND weighted identity reaches the high-confidence threshold.
partialThe full-length criteria fail, but global coverage reaches the partial threshold.
degraded_relicNeither of the above applies.

Both coverage sliders apply to the same coverage measure. A high-coverage, lower-identity match can be partial. A fragmented match can be full-length by these thresholds; the label is not proof of an intact or active TE.

The cards recalculate all reported copies, independently of the resident table filters. Discovery filters remain fixed and pipeline files remain unchanged.

Multiple matches versus multiple families

Copies with multiple candidate matches have more than one retained match, possibly from the same family. Copies with multiple candidate families are the subset with more than one distinct TE label.

Two matches labelled roo contribute to the first counter. Matches labelled roo and copia contribute to both. Alternative labels are not automatically confirmed composite alleles. The primary annotation supplies the copy's reported coverage and identity.

Structure · Provisional copy relations

These counts describe pairs of overlapping copy intervals, not numbers of distinct copies or raw HSPs. One copy may participate in several relations. For fragmented matches, genomic intervals can span unaligned gaps.

Exact report termMeaning
same_family_overlapTwo overlapping copies have the same primary TE label. This test takes precedence over containment.
nested_candidateTwo copies have different primary labels and one genomic interval lies fully inside the other. This is a containment hypothesis, not confirmed biological nesting.
overlap_candidateTwo copies with different primary labels overlap without full containment.

Global counts and threshold preview

All reported relations always retains the complete baseline. The independent preview requires both endpoint copies to reach the minimum primary-match identity and minimum consensus coverage. At 0% / 0%, no additional threshold is applied to the discovered catalogue.

Changing the preview only filters existing relation pairs; it does not rerun BLAST, choose new primary families, regroup copies or change relation types. A relation whose primary label fails a threshold is excluded even if an alternative annotation passes. The preview is independent of call-table and resident-table filters.

Candidate evidence and source genomic alignments

Evidence fraction is a share of preserved candidate evidence, not allele frequency or necessarily a fraction of unique reads. Several alignments or extraction channels can originate from the same read.

Source genomic alignments (queries) counts extracted query sequences by the SAM status of their source alignment: primary, secondary, supplementary, or both secondary and supplementary. These describe the source genomic alignment, not BLAST ranking or independent TE support. vcf_reported sequences have no single assigned source read; missing older metadata remain unknown.

Coordinates, exports and sharing

BED coordinates are zero-based and half-open. Reference-projected coordinates, reconstructed-genome coordinates and query/consensus coordinates are different systems. Nearby calls do not by themselves establish a shared biological locus.

The main report.html embeds its data and scripts. Keep report-help.html beside it when sharing so the contextual Help links remain available. Both pages work without a server or external assets. Filtered TSV downloads change no pipeline files.
